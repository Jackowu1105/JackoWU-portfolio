## Research & Insights

I conducted **stakeholder interviews** (8 sessions with building managers and executives), **tenant interviews** (15 in-depth sessions), **competitive analysis** of 6 premium office apps globally (WeWork, Equinox, The Great Room), and a **survey** (120 responses from tenants).

### Key Findings

- **Multiple user groups, different needs:** Employees need quick access to building services; visitors need simple check-in; admins need oversight capabilities; merchants need exposure for loyalty program
- **Mobile-first mindset:** 89% of tenants prefer mobile app over web portal
- **Trust & transparency matter:** Users want real-time status updates and visibility into who processed their requests
- **Loyalty program had potential:** 82% interested in earning points from building merchants

## Design Process

### Information Architecture

Organized 4 major feature sets into tab-based navigation: **Home** (dashboard with quick actions), **Services** (request management), **Access** (Smart Pass and Visitor Management), **Rewards** (loyalty program and merchant directory). Used progressive disclosure to keep the main interface clean.

### Key Design Decisions

**QR Code for Smart Pass:** No additional hardware required, works offline, easy to issue and revoke remotely. QR code refreshes every 60 seconds for security.

**Visitor Management Redesign:** Transformed a 10-15 minute manual check-in process into a 30-second digital flow — tenants pre-register visitors, generate QR code, guard scans on arrival.

**Gamified Loyalty:** Visual progress bars, one-tap redemption at point of sale, points earned for building services engagement. Participation increased from 15% to 52%.

### Usability Testing

**Round 1 (Low-Fidelity, 8 participants):** 5/8 couldn't find Visitor Management feature (buried too deep). Moved to top-level "Access" tab. Added onboarding screens explaining loyalty program.

**Round 2 (High-Fidelity, 12 participants):** 11/12 completed all primary tasks successfully. SUS Score: 82/100 (Grade A). Average task completion time: 2.5 minutes (vs. 10-15 minutes with old process).

## Visual Design

**Design System:** 35+ reusable components with light/dark mode support. Color palette: Primary Blue (#0066CC) for trust, Secondary Teal (#00B8A9) for modern premium feel. Bilingual support for Thai and English.

**Accessibility:** 44x44pt minimum touch targets, 4.5:1 color contrast ratio, iOS Dynamic Type support, VoiceOver labels for all interactive elements.

## Impact

The app achieved **78% tenant adoption** across 3 buildings, **62% faster request resolution** (48h to 18h), **58% reduction in front desk call volume**, and **4.6/5 App Store rating**. The loyalty program saw a **247% increase in participation** (15% to 52%). Featured in Thailand Property Awards 2021 (Innovation Category).
