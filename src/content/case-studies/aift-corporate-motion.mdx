## Problem Definition

A company's motion presence spans from the serious (a corporate brand video) to the festive (Christmas and Lunar New Year greetings). Without a consistent motion language, the corporate film and the seasonal GIFs feel like they come from different companies.

### Design Goals

- **One motion language across corporate and festive** — consistent pacing, transitions, and visual style
- **Corporate brand video** — the company story in motion, credible and forward-looking
- **Seasonal campaigns** — Christmas 2024/2025 and Lunar New Year 2026, warm and social-ready

## Design Process

### Motion Language

I established the motion language first — the transition style, pacing, and visual treatment that would carry across both corporate and festive content. Then I applied it to each piece, letting the content differ while the motion stayed consistent.

### Materials

- **2026 corporate brand video** — the company story in motion, the flagship piece

## Visual Design

### Color

A muted, warm neutral system — soft sage (`#E0E1D3` / `#A2A599`) with a muted blue-grey (`#667077`) and a deep navy (`#213058`) for type and structure. The palette is calm enough for the corporate film and warm enough for the festive campaigns.

## Reflections

Motion design across corporate and festive is about finding the thread that holds the brand together across very different emotional registers. The motion language — pacing, transitions, visual treatment — is what makes a corporate film and a Christmas GIF feel like they come from the same company. Getting that language right up front meant every piece was a variation on a theme, not a fresh start.
