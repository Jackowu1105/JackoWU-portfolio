## Overview

AIFT's 2026 corporate brand video tells the company story in motion — who the lab is, the work it does, and the direction it's heading. Designed as a single, cohesive film, it moves through AIFT's story with one consistent motion language: steady pacing, clean transitions, and a restrained visual treatment that keeps the company feeling credible and forward-looking.

## Design Direction

- **One motion language** — pacing, transitions, and visual treatment stay consistent throughout, holding the film together as a single company story
- **Muted warm neutral palette** — soft sage (`#E0E1D3` / `#A2A599`) with blue-grey (`#667077`) and deep navy (`#213058`), calm enough to be credible for a corporate film
- **Flagship brand film** — the 2026 corporate video as the centrepiece, telling AIFT's story at a watchable, shareable length
