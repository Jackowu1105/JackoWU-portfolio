## Overview

A motion graphics project spanning the AIFT corporate brand video (2026) and seasonal campaigns — Christmas 2024/2025 and Lunar New Year 2026. The work centres on one motion language: consistent pacing, transitions, and visual treatment that hold a serious company film and quick festive greetings together as one brand. Each piece is a variation on that theme.

## Design Direction

- **One motion language, different content** — pacing, transitions, and visual treatment stay consistent; only the content shifts, from the company story to festive warmth
- **Muted warm neutral palette** — soft sage (`#E0E1D3` / `#A2A599`) with blue-grey (`#667077`) and deep navy (`#213058`), calm enough for the corporate film and warm enough for the campaigns
- **Flagship plus seasonal variations** — the 2026 corporate brand video as the centrepiece, with Christmas and Lunar New Year greetings as social-ready variations on the same system
