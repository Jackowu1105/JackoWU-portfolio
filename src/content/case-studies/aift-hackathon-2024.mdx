## Overview

AIFT's 2024 hackathon needed a visual identity spanning a 2048×1280 LED wall backdrop, entrance TV signage in multiple resolutions, certificates, and a logo lockup. I designed a blue-purple tech system flexible enough to carry the event from a wall-size display to a printed certificate — energetic for a hackathon, credible for an AIFT-branded event.

## Design Direction

- **Blue-purple tech system** — a bright sky blue, deep blue and vivid purple gradient that reads as forward-looking and tech-forward, not corporate
- **Legibility at distance** — bold display type and a strong hierarchy that hold up on the LED wall and entrance signage from across the hall
- **One system, many configurations** — LED wall backdrop, entrance signage, certificates, and logo lockup all read as one event because each material is a configuration of the same system
