## Research & Insights

I conducted **stakeholder interviews** with building managers and executives, **tenant interviews**, **competitive analysis** of premium office apps (WeWork, Equinox, The Great Room), and a **tenant survey**.

### Key Findings

- **Multiple user groups, different needs** — employees need quick access to building services; visitors need simple check-in; admins need oversight; merchants want exposure for the loyalty program
- **Mobile-first mindset** — most tenants prefer a mobile app over a web portal
- **Trust & transparency matter** — users want real-time status updates and visibility into who processed their requests
- **Loyalty program had potential** — many tenants were interested in earning points from building merchants

## Design Process

### Information Architecture

Organised four major feature sets into tab-based navigation: **Home** (dashboard with quick actions), **Services** (request management), **Access** (Smart Pass and Visitor Management), and **Rewards** (loyalty program and merchant directory). I used progressive disclosure to keep the main interface clean.

### Key Design Decisions

**QR code for Smart Pass** — No additional hardware required, works offline, and easy to issue and revoke remotely. The QR refreshes periodically for security.

**Visitor management redesign** — Transformed a slow manual check-in process into a quick digital flow: tenants pre-register visitors, generate a QR code, and the guard scans it on arrival.

**Gamified loyalty** — Visual progress bars, one-tap redemption at point of sale, and points earned for engaging with building services. Participation rose notably.

### Usability Testing

**Round 1 (low-fidelity)** — Several participants couldn't find the Visitor Management feature (it was buried too deep). I moved it to a top-level "Access" tab and added onboarding screens explaining the loyalty program.

**Round 2 (high-fidelity)** — Most participants completed all primary tasks successfully. The System Usability Scale (SUS) score landed in the top grade range, and task completion time fell sharply versus the old manual process.

## Visual Design

### Design System

A reusable component library with light/dark mode support.

**Color:** A light blue-grey surface (`#EDF0F5` / `#D6DFE8`) for a clean, premium feel, with a dark slate (`#454549`) for text and primary actions. The palette keeps the interface calm and professional, suited to a premium office-building audience.

**Typography:** A system sans-serif with a clear hierarchy, supporting bilingual Thai and English.

### Accessibility

Generous minimum touch targets, 4.5:1 color contrast ratio, iOS Dynamic Type support, and VoiceOver labels for all interactive elements.

## Outcomes & Feedback

The app unified maintenance requests, QR access, visitor management, and a loyalty program into one experience; tenant adoption was strong, request resolution sped up, front-desk call volume dropped, and the loyalty program's participation grew markedly. The project was recognised in a property-industry awards programme.

## Reflections

Designing for multiple user groups (tenants, visitors, management, merchants) in one app required finding the "win-win-win" feature — the loyalty program connected tenant engagement, merchant exposure, and management insight at once. Progressive disclosure kept the interface clean despite the breadth of features.
