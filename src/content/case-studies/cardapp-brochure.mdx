## Overview

A printed brochure for CardApp that introduces the residential community card product — what it is, how it works for residents and merchants, and why it belongs in a community. The design works as a single printed document: a clear hierarchy, a consistent visual system, and spreads that read naturally in sequence.

## Design Direction

- **Service-led, not tech-led** — the copy and layout lead with what CardApp does for a community, not with features
- **Clear print hierarchy** — type scale, spacing, and section rhythm carry the reader through 27 pages without losing the thread
- **One visual system, every spread** — consistent colour, type, and layout language hold the whole document together as a single piece
