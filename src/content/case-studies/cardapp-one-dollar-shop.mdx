## Overview

一元購 $1 SHOP is CardApp's recurring in-community campaign — each edition offers residents a single item for $1 at a particular estate. I designed the A4 posters that carry each edition: a layout that names the product, states the $1 offer, and shows the resident how to take part, tuned to the estate and the product of the month.

## Design Direction

- **Single-offer clarity** — one product, one price, one call to action per poster; the $1 promise reads first
- **Product-led, campaign-framed** — each poster leads with its item (fruit-tart pet bed, KF94 mask, CMHK power bank, mooncake…) inside the recognisable 一元購 system
- **Estate-localised editions** — the same recurring campaign adapts to different estates and editions without losing the family look
