## Research & Discovery

I analyzed 5 competitor campaigns identifying common patterns and pain points. Average form abandonment rate was 55%, and most sites asked for too much information upfront. Mobile traffic accounted for 65-75% of visits. I also conducted 8 user interviews with c!ty'super customers.

### User Needs

- "I want to know what I could win before signing up" — need for upfront prize visibility
- "I don't trust these draws are actually random" — need for transparency and trust signals
- "I don't have time to fill out long forms" — need for quick, low-effort participation

## Design Process

### Key Design Decisions

**Progressive Disclosure:** Split the experience into 3 steps — Browse products first (engages users before asking for info), then provide basic info (name + email only), then receive instant confirmation. Research shows users are 3x more likely to complete forms after engaging with content.

**Visual Product Gallery with Smart Filters:** Card-based gallery with high-quality product images, category filters, and multi-select with visual "selected" state. Limited selection to 3 products to prevent decision paralysis.

**Trust-Building Elements:** Live participant counter on landing page, clear T&Cs with draw date, confirmation email with unique entry ID, and "Your entry #XXXX has been recorded" message.

### Wireframe Iterations

- **Concept A** (single-page scroll) — felt too long
- **Concept B** (multi-step wizard) — felt too slow
- **Concept C** (hybrid: sticky product selector + wizard flow) — best feedback ✅

### Visual Design

Maintained c!ty'super's premium brand aesthetic with brand green (#00A650) for primary actions and festive yellow (#FFD700) for promotional energy. Mobile-first approach designed for 375px viewport with touch-friendly targets (min 48×48px).

## Impact

Campaign achieved **12,500 participants** (25% above 10,000 target), **89% form completion rate** (vs. 55% industry average), **72% mobile conversion rate**, and collected **12,500 email addresses** for marketing database. Website traffic increased 35% during the campaign period. The design system was reused for 3 subsequent promotional campaigns.

## Reflections

Progressive disclosure reduces friction dramatically. Trust signals matter — the live participant counter and unique entry IDs significantly improved user confidence. Reducing form fields from 8 to 2 had the biggest single impact on conversion.
