## Overview

Two products, one booth. The InnoEX 2025 presence needed to give FinSightsAI and TOLO each their own voice while reading as a single AIFT presence — and carry that balance across the booth, one A4 leaflet per product, and paired 850×2000 pull-up banners. The solution was to lock the AIFT visual language first, then let every material fall out of the same system.

## Design Direction

- **Two product zones, one AIFT frame** — each product gets its own section and accent inside the shared visual language, so visitors can focus on one product at a time without the booth feeling split
- **Light, airy blue system** — soft sky blue (`#D9EEFA` / `#A4D5F1`) surfaces with confident mid-blue (`#5EB7E5` / `#6994CC`) accents, consistent across both products
- **One system across print and signage** — consistent type, colour, and hierarchy carried from booth to A4 leaflets to pull-up banners, legible at distance
