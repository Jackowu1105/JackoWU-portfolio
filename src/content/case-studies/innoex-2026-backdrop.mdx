## Overview

A large-format refresh of the AIFT exhibition presence: the InnoEX 2026 booth backdrop carries the brand forward from 2025 on a cool blue base, with a warm coral accent as the signal that something is new. The direction was set as refresh with continuity — impact at wall scale, recognisably AIFT. V1 is in progress.

## Design Direction

- **Cool blue base, warm coral accent** — the blue (`#D5D9E7` / `#ACBEDB` / `#6895C7`) carries 2025 brand equity forward; coral (`#E39972`) adds energy and warmth as the 2026 signal
- **Refresh without breaking the brand** — a new direction that stays part of the same system, not a repeat of last year and not a departure
- **Large-format impact** — bold display type and a pairing refined to read clearly from across the hall at wall scale
