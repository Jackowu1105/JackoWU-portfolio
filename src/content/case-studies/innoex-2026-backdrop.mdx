## Problem Definition

An exhibition backdrop is the event's main visual anchor — it's what visitors see from across the hall and what frames the booth experience. The 2026 backdrop needed to carry AIFT forward from 2025 with a fresh direction while staying recognisably part of the same brand.

### Design Goals

- **Fresh direction from 2025** — a cool blue base with a warm coral accent, a new visual direction
- **Large-format impact** — readable and impactful at wall scale
- **Brand continuity** — recognisably AIFT, even with the new accent

## Design Process

### Visual Direction

I explored directions that would refresh the 2025 blue system without breaking brand continuity. The warm coral accent (`#E39972`) is the key shift — it adds energy and warmth against the cool blue base, giving the 2026 backdrop its own identity while the blue keeps it grounded in the AIFT brand.

## Visual Design

### Color

A cool blue base (`#D5D9E7` / `#ACBEDB` / `#6895C7`) with a warm coral accent (`#E39972`) — the blue carries the AIFT brand continuity, the coral is the fresh 2026 direction. The pairing reads as modern and energetic against the 2025 system.

### Typography

A bold sans-serif with a strong hierarchy — large display weights for the AIFT brand and product line, legible at distance on the large-format backdrop.

## Reflections

Refreshing an exhibition presence year over year is a balance of continuity and new direction. The coral accent is the 2026 signal — it tells visitors something is new without throwing away the brand equity built in 2025. V1 is still in progress; the system will evolve as the event approaches.
