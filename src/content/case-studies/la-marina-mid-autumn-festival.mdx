## Overview

For 海上居 LA MARINA's Mid-Autumn Festival Carnival 2019 — a Macau estate community event under the CardApp programme — I designed the full campaign collateral: the main festival poster, redemption-booth signage, the game rules, a top banner, and the in-app information that told residents about the event. Print and app read as one 中秋嘉年華 system, so the celebration carried from the notice board to the phone screen without losing its festive identity.

## Design Direction

- **One 中秋嘉年華 system across print and app** — the main poster, booth signage, rules, banner, and in-app screens all read as a single event identity
- **Festive and legible** — the Mid-Autumn warmth comes through without burying the practical details (date, place, how to take part)
- **App as part of the campaign, not an afterthought** — the in-app information carries the same visual language so residents meet the same event in both places
