  ## Problem Definition

A smart massage gun is only as good as the guidance around it. Without instruction, users apply it incorrectly — wrong intensity, wrong muscle group, too long — which limits the benefit and risks injury. The companion app needed to solve three things at once:

- **Safe usage** — clear, step-by-step guidance so first-time users don't hurt themselves
- **Personalisation** — therapy programs adaptable to different bodies, goals, and recovery states
- **Progress visibility** — a way to see what's been done and what's improving over time

### Design Goals

- Make correct usage feel guided, not gated — instructions should help, not slow users down
- Let casual users start quickly while giving advanced users room to customise
- Keep the interface calm and wellness-oriented, not clinical

## Design Process

### Information Architecture

I structured the app around four primary sections:

1. **Home** — quick-start with recent programs and recommended sessions
2. **Programs** — a library of therapy programs (recovery, pain relief, relaxation) with customisable intensity, mode, and target area
3. **Guide** — step-by-step usage tutorials with safety precautions
4. **Progress** — session history and improvement tracking

### Key Design Decisions

**Guided usage instructions.** The app provides comprehensive tutorials on how to use the massage gun correctly — step-by-step guidance with safety precautions, so users build confidence before they start.

**Personalised therapy programs.** A library of programs addresses different needs (muscle recovery, pain relief, relaxation). Users can customise intensity levels, massage modes, and target areas to suit their preferences and requirements.

**Customisation without clutter.** Advanced settings (intensity, mode, target area) live one tap away from the main control, so first-time users see a simple surface while power users can dial in their session.

**Progress tracking.** Users can track their therapy sessions and monitor progress over time, enabling them to adjust settings and see improvements — turning a one-off device into a habit.

## Visual Design

### Design System

A clean, calm wellness aesthetic.

**Color:** A light base (`#FFFFFF` / `#F4F5F6`) with a confident blue primary (`#187CDA` / `#0778D7`) for actions and active states, softened by a pale blue (`#C3D4F1` / `#DBE9EF`) for secondary surfaces. The palette feels trustworthy and wellness-oriented without being clinical.

<ColorPalette

**Typography:** A system sans-serif with a clear hierarchy — large, legible controls for in-session use (users may be mid-massage and glancing at the screen).

### Accessibility

Generous touch targets for mid-session use, high-contrast controls, and clear status indicators for device connection and intensity.

## Outcomes & Feedback

The Smart Massage Gun paired with its app gave users a comprehensive, personalised massage therapy experience — accurate usage instructions reduced misuse anxiety, and customisable programs meant the device served a wide range of recovery needs rather than a single fixed routine.

## Reflections

Designing for a physical IoT device means the interface has to account for the moment of use — users glance at the screen mid-session, so controls need to be large, legible, and reachable. Personalisation only works when the default path is already good; the customisation is there for users who want it, not a burden on those who don't.
