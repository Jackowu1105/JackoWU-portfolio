## Overview

A MOMAX marketing event campaign needed a full suite of materials — Facebook posts, campaign posters, collectible tokens, app banners, an EV charger backdrop, an A5 leaflet, and A4 posters. I established one visual language up front and produced every piece as a configuration of the system, keeping each touchpoint recognisable from social feed to large-format print.

## Design Direction

- **MOMAX blue leads the identity** — brand blue (#0195E2) against clean white with deep blue for structure, inheriting the product line's visual identity so the campaign reads as MOMAX instantly
- **Bold headline type + core motif** — readable at distance on the backdrop, scroll-stopping on social, with the motif constant across every surface
- **One system, many configurations** — colour, motif and typography stay constant while the layout adapts to each surface's constraints — a Facebook post and a three-metre backdrop are different compositions of the same identity
