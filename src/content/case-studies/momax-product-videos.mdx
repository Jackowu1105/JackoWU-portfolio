## Overview

Product demo videos sit at the top of the sales funnel — on product pages, in app-store previews, and on social feeds that autoplay on mute. Each cut has a few seconds to convince: the hook lands in the first three seconds, the device shows what it promises, and motion graphics carry the message where footage alone can't.

## Design Direction

- **The product is the star** — motion graphics explain, they never compete with the device on screen
- **Works on mute** — social feeds autoplay without sound, so captions and graphics carry the message
- **First three seconds decide** — the hook appears immediately; details follow in the body of the cut
