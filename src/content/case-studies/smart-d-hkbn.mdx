## Problem Definition

HKBN wanted its smart home app to be a true ecosystem — one place to control every device a customer owns, including third-party hardware. MOMAX Smart devices were a natural fit: strong hardware, but no bridge into HKBN's platform.

The work split into two tracks:

- **Product integration** — designing how MOMAX devices appear, pair, and get controlled inside the HKBN app, including voice control via Google Assistant and Alexa
- **Project management** — managing Smart D's IoT IP product production timelines and submitting designs with HKBN so hardware and software stayed in step

### Design Goals

- **Make MOMAX devices feel native to HKBN** — not a bolt-on, but an integrated part of the ecosystem
- **Voice control as a first-class citizen** — not just in-app control, but Google Assistant and Alexa too
- **Keep hardware and software timelines aligned** — a design decision made today shouldn't stall a production line tomorrow

## Design Process

### Integration Design

Designing for an ecosystem means designing *against* your own assumptions. MOMAX devices had their own pairing flow, naming conventions, and control surfaces — but inside the HKBN app they had to feel native to HKBN. I worked through:

- **Pairing and onboarding** — how a MOMAX device enters the HKBN ecosystem smoothly, without two-step brand confusion for the customer
- **Control surfaces** — device cards, toggles, and status states that match HKBN's design language while still reading clearly as MOMAX hardware
- **Voice control** — mapping device states and scenes so Google Assistant and Alexa can control the same devices the app controls

### Coordination & Project Management

Hardware timelines don't wait for design iterations. I managed Smart D's IoT product production schedules and submitted designs with HKBN on a cadence that kept both sides moving — so a design decision made today wouldn't stall a production line tomorrow.

## Visual Design

### Design System

The integration follows the host app's system: clean, light surfaces with MOMAX devices presented as native HKBN app elements.

**Color:** Light neutral surfaces (`#FFFFFF` / `#EDF0F5`) with blue-grey accents (`#D6DFE8`) and dark slate (`#454549`) for text — letting the host app's brand lead while device states stay clearly readable.

## Reflections

Ecosystem integration taught me that design decisions ripple across two companies' timelines. The interesting part wasn't making a device card look native — it was sequencing design work so hardware production and software integration never blocked each other. Voice control was the unlock: once a device answers to Google Assistant and Alexa, it stops being "a MOMAX device" and becomes simply part of the home.
