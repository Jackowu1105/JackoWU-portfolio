## Overview

The Smart Logistics Datathon 2024 is a competition co-organised by AISCL and GOVHK, sitting between a hackathon and an official government-backed event. I designed the event's visual identity as one system — backdrop, roll-up, rundown, and certificate — that balances an official, credible tone with the energy of a competition.

## Design Direction

- **Confident blue + vivid pink-red accent** — official enough for the government co-organiser, energetic enough for a datathon, without feeling corporate
- **Movement and data flow** — a visual language around logistics networks that reads as modern and data-forward
- **One system, many configurations** — the backdrop as the main anchor, the roll-up for distance legibility, the rundown for information hierarchy, and the certificate as the small-scale keepsake
