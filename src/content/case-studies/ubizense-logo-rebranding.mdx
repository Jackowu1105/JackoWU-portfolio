## Overview

The brief was to give ubiZense a logo and brand system that could hold up at every scale. The redesigned mark — built on a sensing-inspired concept — anchors a complete style guide covering colour, typography, and construction rules, so every downstream application is a system output rather than a special case.

## Design Direction

- **Sensing concept** — the mark distils detection, signal, and awareness into a single recognisable form
- **Built on a construction grid** — geometric rules make the logo reproducible and scalable from favicon to backdrop
- **One system, every touchpoint** — name cards, website, and event materials all read as the same brand
